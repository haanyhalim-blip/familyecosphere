Family Ecosphere

Everyday tools for you and your family.

Small, free helpers that each do one job well. Use one, or use them together. No app to install – they work in your phone's browser.

🌱 We're building this with you…

These apps have one job: to be a handy, on-the-go helper for you and your family. That's it.

They keep growing based on what you need. If there's anything that would make them more helpful, let us know. We read every message, and we usually add things quickly.

No account needed.

How they fit together

Morning: DoByToday shows what's next, in time order – for you, or for everyone at home.

Before you leave: PackByBag has the school bag, the sports kit and the trip bag ready to check.

At the shop: ListByAisle sorts the list the way the shop is laid out. Good Food for Kids gives you ideas to put on it.

After school: LearnByYear shows what this year's learning looks like, so you can help in small ways.

Evening: WatchByValues helps choose a film together, with moments worth talking about after.

For you: ResetbyChoice is a quiet page of your own to build one better habit.

Sign in once and you stay signed in as you move between them. You can also ask your own Claude or ChatGPT to update your lists.
